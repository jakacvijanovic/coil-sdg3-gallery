Rooftop Rush

Lead a rooftop runner through four burger ambushes and a final showdown with the King Stack. Fire peas, dodge ketchup shots and collect salad to restore one heart.

This game requires a browser with canvas support.

Move with WASD or the arrows. Hold Space to fire at the nearest burger, or hold the mouse button to aim. Tap Shift to dash in your movement direction. A dash blocks damage briefly and recharges in 3 seconds. Press P to pause.
