The goal

Health at every age

SDG 3 calls on countries to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for everyone. It covers prevention, mental health, road safety and access to essential health services.

Distance and cost can put care out of reach. Someone who cannot travel to a clinic may delay a visit until a manageable problem becomes more serious. Affordable local services and reliable transport can close that gap.

Health in everyday places

Movement · Road safety

The route to school

A busy crossing can make a short walk feel unsafe. Slower traffic, clear crossings and continuous pavements give more people the option to walk or cycle.

Environment · Well-being

A place to pause

A quiet green space offers room to move, rest or meet a friend. Accessible paths, shade and seating help people of different ages enjoy the same place.

Care · Equal access

Within reach

A clinic across town may be difficult to reach without a car. A bus stop near the entrance or a visiting health team can bring essential services closer to home.
